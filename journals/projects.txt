Each project in this journal will be organized under a different heading. Some projects may span several sections. 

# Gaslamp Fantasy

A story that progressively becomes more of a cosmic horror story that draws from various modern sources of gaslamp fantasy and is informed by the little mundane way of life steadily becoming more horrorific and indefinite. 

We discover a society that is heavily stratified within its own respective classes and skilled trades. There is a complex caste system that has been in place for a long time but it is beginning to show signs of decay even as the world around it begins to undergo similar urban decay. As the series goes on, it should become apparent that this is a process that is gradually getting faster.
