## Universe

These stories exist in a dynamically expanding universe. There are a lot of areas that are under continued development. These stories grow organically and each story should be, largely, self-contained and only expand on the lore and world-building. 

## Worlds

As a space-based universe, there are going to be many worlds that are explored in-depth throughout the stories. Worlds are places with civilization and structures. Planets are worlds that are -- or appear to be -- largely devoid of life. 

* [List of Planets](list_of_planets.md)
* [List of Worlds](list_of_worlds.md)
## Spacecraft

Spacecraft will also feature prominently as locations that stories will be taking place on.  Many of them can use advanced propulsion systems that their cultures, worlds, and developers have created allowing faster-than-light travel.

* [List of Spacecraft](list_of_spacecraft.md)
## Cultures

Cultures will be explored as deeply as possible and in addition to characters backgrounds and life experiences will be a driving force in the stories. 

* [List of Cultures](list_of_cultures.md)

## Technology

Ships use background radiation collection to convert to propulsion power. There will be very advanced races and those that are not as advanced but this should be more a reflection of the very definition of technology rather than an elitist claim, unless of course, that's the power structures we're exploring in the text.
